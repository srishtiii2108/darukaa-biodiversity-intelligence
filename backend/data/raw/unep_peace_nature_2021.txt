Human impacts, including deforestation and chemical pollution, severely degrade ecosystem services. In agricultural zones, heavy reliance on synthetic pesticides alters soil pH and diminishes soil microbial diversity. Shifting towards organic Integrated Pest Management (IPM) and applying biochar can neutralize soil pH. This transition provides porous carbon structures for microbial recolonization. Ecosystem recovery is observable within 2-3 years, showcasing enhanced habitat diversity. Furthermore, replacing monoculture with mixed-cropping systems stabilizes the ecological niches needed for both insect and avian biodiversity.
